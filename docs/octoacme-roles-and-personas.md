# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Collaborate with QA/Testing Lead** on acceptance criteria validation, test coverage, and defect resolution
- **Work with Technical Lead/Architect** on design reviews and technical guidance
- **Report to Project Manager** on progress and blockers
- **Implement requirements from Product Manager** based on acceptance criteria

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Align with Stakeholders/Sponsors** on business goals and success metrics
- **Define requirements for Developers** through acceptance criteria
- **Collaborate with Project Manager** on timelines and dependencies
- **Partner with Technical Lead/Architect** on feasibility and scope trade-offs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Report to and escalate with Stakeholders/Sponsors** on project status and blockers
- **Coordinate with QA/Testing Lead** on quality gates and release readiness
- **Track Developers** on task progress and dependencies
- **Facilitate alignment** between Product Manager, Technical Lead/Architect, and delivery team

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy and execution for projects. They define testing approaches, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and QA strategies for each project phase
- Validate acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Execute manual QA and coordinate automated testing efforts
- Identify and document defects, regressions, and edge cases
- Collaborate with developers on testability and coverage
- Participate in release verification and smoke tests

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality products that meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce production defects and customer-impacting issues
- Enable fast iteration through efficient testing practices

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and QA strategy reviews
- Defect tracking and triage meetings
- Pre-release verification and sign-off

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Work with Developers** to understand acceptance criteria, resolve defects, and improve testability
- **Validate requirements from Product Manager** through acceptance criteria review
- **Report to Project Manager** on quality metrics, blockers, and release readiness
- **Collaborate with Technical Lead/Architect** on test strategy for complex components
- **Coordinate with Stakeholders/Sponsors** on release quality gates and acceptance

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, investment decisions, and strategic alignment for projects. They may represent customers, business leadership, or dependent teams.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals and success metrics
- Provide approvals and funding decisions
- Escalate risks and blockers at the sponsor level
- Participate in project kickoff and milestone reviews
- Communicate project status to broader organization
- Validate that solutions meet business needs

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain strategic alignment with organizational priorities
- Enable rapid decision-making and risk resolution

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Milestone reviews and demos
- Escalation paths for business-critical decisions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Set direction with Product Manager** on business goals and prioritization
- **Receive project status from Project Manager** and approve major decisions
- **Participate in reviews with Developers and QA/Testing Lead** to validate deliverables
- **Guide Technical Lead/Architect** on business constraints and technical trade-offs
- **Provide decision authority** for escalated risks and resource constraints

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects define technical strategy, guide design decisions, and ensure solutions align with system architecture and technical standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture for projects
- Review and guide technical design decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers and guide code quality standards
- Ensure solutions scale, perform, and maintain consistency
- Coordinate across teams on technical dependencies

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and system complexity
- Enable knowledge sharing and team growth

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and technical guidance
- Cross-team dependency coordination

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Guide Developers** on design decisions and code quality standards
- **Advise Product Manager** on technical feasibility and scope trade-offs
- **Inform Project Manager** on technical dependencies and risks
- **Partner with QA/Testing Lead** on test strategy for complex components
- **Report to Stakeholders/Sponsors** on technical risks and mitigation strategies
- **Mentor team** on technical standards and best practices

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When working with multiple personas, refer to the "Interactions with Other Roles" sections to understand cross-functional collaboration points.
